☷ Steps

Follow the recipe step by step.

1

Prepare Brussel Sprouts

Slice the brussel sprouts into thin pieces.

2

Wash & Dry

Wash the vegetables thoroughly and let them dry.

3

Mix Ingredients

Add vegetables, walnuts, berries and cheese.

4

Season

Add salt and pepper according to taste.

5

Drizzle & Toss

Add olive oil and gently toss everything together.

6

Serve

Serve fresh and enjoy your healthy salad.
