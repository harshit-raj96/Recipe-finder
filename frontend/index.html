Healthy Brussel Sprout Salad

A fresh, crunchy and nutritious salad made with brussel sprouts, vegetables, nuts, berries and cheese. Perfect for a healthy meal!

◷
Prep Time 15 mins
🍲
Cook Time 10 mins
▮
Difficulty Easy
🍴
Servings 2-3

🌿 Nutritional Information (Per Serving)

Calories 320 kcal
Protein 12 g
Carbs 28 g
Fats 18 g
Fiber 7 g
1

Prepare Brussel Sprouts

Slice the brussel sprouts using a mandoline or sharp knife into thin pieces.

2

Wash & Dry

Wash the sliced brussel sprouts thoroughly and let them dry using a clean towel.

3

Mix Ingredients

Add brussel sprouts, lemon juice, roasted walnut, dried berries and shaved cheese.

4

Season

Add salt and pepper according to your taste.

5

Drizzle & Toss

Drizzle olive oil over the salad and gently toss everything together.

6

Serve

Serve fresh and enjoy your healthy brussel sprout salad.

♡ Health Benefits

  • ✓ Brussel sprouts are rich in vitamins C and K.
  • ✓ High in fiber and antioxidants.
  • ✓ Walnuts provide healthy omega-3 fats.
  • ✓ Berries contain useful antioxidants.
  • ✓ Olive oil provides healthy fats.

💡 Cooking Tips

  • Use fresh brussel sprouts for better texture and taste.
  • You can roast the vegetables for a warm version.
  • Add grilled chicken or chickpeas for extra protein.
